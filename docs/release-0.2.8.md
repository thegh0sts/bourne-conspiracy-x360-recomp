**The launcher's settings are now in tabs.** A launcher-only update. You need your own copy of the game (Xbox 360 disc image); nothing from the game is included.

**On 0.2.7?** Click **Download and update** in the launcher (or **Check for updates**), or download `BourneConspiracy.exe` and run it. **No reinstall is needed**: the game itself is the same as 0.2.7. Coming from 0.2.6 or earlier, reinstall as the launcher asks, for 0.2.7's changes. Your settings are kept.

### Changes
- **Settings in tabs**: Game files, Display, Graphics, Camera and Controls down the left, the chosen tab's settings on the right, in a wider, shorter window instead of one long page. The reinstall notice and Play / Quit / Defaults stay on every tab, and the launcher reopens on the last tab you used.
  - **Camera** holds field of view and the camera options from 0.2.7.
  - **Controls** holds keyboard, controller and mouse settings together, the mouse ones under their own heading.
