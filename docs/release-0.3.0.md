**Quit Game on the main menu, a detailed performance overlay, and new Saves and Achievements tabs in the launcher.** You need your own copy of the game (Xbox 360 disc image); nothing from the game is included.

**On 0.2.8?** Click **Download and update** in the launcher (or download `BourneConspiracy.exe` and run it), then click **Reinstall now** on the red notice. The game is rebuilt and the bundled SDK is replaced (`tbc10`). Your settings and saves are kept.

### In the game
- **Quit Game** on the main menu, under Cheats, in the game's language. Select it and press A / Enter to close the game.
- **Detailed performance overlay**: the launcher's FPS counter is now **Off / FPS / Detailed**. Detailed adds frame time, GPU time and load, CPU load (PC and game), RAM and video memory, and a frame-time graph. Needs the native renderer.
- **The FPS counter shows in fullscreen** with the native renderer: it's drawn into the picture now, so it also appears in screenshots and recordings.
- **Achievement pop-ups drawn by the native renderer**, same look as before, without slowing the game down while one shows. They now appear in screenshots and recordings too.
- **Brightness** slider (native renderer).
- **Controller vibration strength** slider, 0-100% (0 is off).
- **Keyboard prompts in every language**: German, French, Spanish and Italian menus and fight tutorials show your keys too.

### In the launcher
- **Achievements tab**: every achievement with its icon, which you've unlocked and when, and your gamerscore. It's there straight after installing, and follows the launcher's language.
- **Saves tab**: your saved games with their names, dates and sizes, plus Open saves folder and Back up saves.
- **Game language**: English, German, French, Spanish or Italian (voices, subtitles and text).
- **Hide achievement pop-ups** checkbox.
- **Save** button: keeps your settings without starting the game, and asks before quitting with unsaved changes.
- Graphics path and Anti-aliasing only show with the Xenos renderer; links are now small red buttons.

### Removed
- The unused ReGPU renderer experiment.

Native renderer by the [Skullburglar](https://github.com/nlwstein/skullburglar) project (BSD 3-Clause).
