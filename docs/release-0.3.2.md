**Keyboard and mouse for every takedown in the second level, a middle-mouse glyph, and Quit Game only on the main menu.** You need your own copy of the game (Xbox 360 disc image); nothing from the game is included.

**Updating?** Click **Download and update** in the launcher (or download `BourneConspiracy.exe` and run it), then click **Reinstall now** on the red notice. The game is rebuilt; the bundled SDK stays `tbc10`. Your settings and saves are kept.

### New
- **Shooting takedowns work with keyboard and mouse.** Press the key in the same place as the button on the prompt's diamond: W (top), A (left), S (bottom), D (right).
- **Middle mouse button glyph** in prompts bound to it (recentre the camera, switch shoulder).
- **Aim assist checkbox** (launcher, Controls tab): the game's sticky aim is off with keyboard and mouse unless you tick it. It's made for a stick and drags the camera against the mouse.

### Fixes
- **Multi-takedown and boss takedown prompts sometimes ignored the keys**: the first takedown of each kind in a session, and every takedown after the boat sequence.
- **Quit Game showed under Replay Chapter's list.** It's on the main menu only now.

### Known issue
- **Above 60 FPS (experimental), scripted moments can stall** in the second level, some of the time: Solomon's intro in the warehouse may never start (he stands still and can't be hurt), and on the boat one enemy of the second wave may stay on the support ship. Load the last checkpoint, or play at 60. We're working on it.
