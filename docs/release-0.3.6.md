**Controller camera fixes and an aiming reticle size option.** You need your own copy of the game (Xbox 360 disc image); nothing from the game is included.

**Updating?** Click **Download and update** in the launcher (or download `BourneConspiracy.exe` and run it). This is a launcher-only update: no reinstall needed. Your settings and saves are kept.

### Fixes
- **Stick turn speed now works at full stick** (launcher, Controls tab). With the stick pushed all the way, the camera turned at the game's own fixed top speed whatever the slider said; the slider now raises that top speed too.
- **Linear stick turning** (Camera tab) left the camera stuck at its slow starting speed. It now turns at full speed straight away, with no acceleration.

### New
- **Stick camera smoothing** (Controls tab, 100% = the game's): lower it for a snappier, more responsive camera on a controller.
- **Custom aiming reticle size** (Camera tab, off by default): tick it to show a size slider, 50-250%. Unticked keeps the game's own size.
